# BadApple-PICO-8
A collection of programs to play Bad Apple on the PICO-8


This collection comprises of a few scripts to convert, compress, encode and play Bad Apple on the PICO-8.


The video is played at 16x12 pixels and 4 FPS because of the PICO-8 character limit. I ended up using 97% of the compressed cartridge capacity.
I have plans on making a better encoder later, but this already took me about 4 days, those scripts that I put here were made in just a few hours.
